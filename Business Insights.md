# 1. Banking Overview

### Key Findings
- Total Branches: 50
- Total Customers: 5,000
- Total Accounts: 7,000
- Total Transactions: 50,000
- Total Transaction Amount: 437.66M
- Total Fraud Transactions: 1,026
- Total Fraud Amount: 41.47M
- Overall Fraud Rate: 2.05%

### Business Insights
1. The bank serves 5,000 customers across 50 branches, managing a total of 7,000 accounts.
2. A total transaction value of 437.66M indicates significant banking activity across different transaction channels.
3. Out of 50,000 transactions, 1,026 were identified as fraudulent, resulting in an overall fraud rate of 2.05%.
4. The identified fraud amount of 41.47M highlights the financial risk associated with fraudulent transactions.
5. These findings indicate the importance of continuous transaction monitoring and effective fraud prevention mechanisms.

### Recommendations
- **Strengthen Fraud Monitoring:** Implement continuous monitoring to identify suspicious and high-value transactions.
- **Improve Customer Security:** Promote secure banking practices and customer awareness to prevent fraud.
- **Monitor Branch Performance:** Compare branch-wise transaction volumes and fraud rates to identify high-risk branches.
- **Improve Data Quality:** Regularly validate customer, account and transaction records to ensure accurate reporting.

### Business Insights

**1. Gender-wise Analysis**

- Customers classified as Others account for 11.74%, with a fraud rate of 1.80%.

### Recommendations

- **Monitor High-Risk Age Groups:** Pay closer attention to the 46–55 and 56–65 age groups.
- **Improve Customer Data Quality:** Correct missing gender and KYC information to improve customer segmentation.
- **Implement Risk-Based Monitoring:** Use customer profiles and transaction patterns to identify potentially suspicious activities.
- **Improve Customer Awareness:** Educate customers about phishing, identity theft and other common banking fraud methods.

# 4. Fraud Analysis

### Key Findings
- Total Transactions: 50,000
- Total Fraud Transactions: 1,026
- Total Transaction Amount: 437.66M
- Total Fraud Amount: 41.47M
- Successful Fraud Transaction Amount: 16.74M
- Failed Fraud Transaction Amount: 16.38M
- Average Fraud Amount: 40.42K
- Non-Fraud Transaction Amount: 396.19M
- Overall Fraud Rate: 2.05%

### Business Insights

**1. Fraud Type Analysis**
- Phishing: 303 cases (30%).
- Account Takeover: 161 cases (16%).
- SIM Swap: 157 cases (15%).
- Card Skimming: 142 cases (14%).
- Identity Theft: 139 cases (14%).
- Unknown: 124 cases (12%).

Phishing and Account Takeover together account for approximately 46% of fraud cases, making them the two most common identified fraud types.

**2. Payment Method Analysis**
- IMPS: 8,406 transactions, 182 fraudulent (2.17%).
- NEFT: 16,772 transactions, 347 fraudulent (2.07%).
- UPI: 16,435 transactions, 332 fraudulent (2.02%).
- RTGS: 8,387 transactions, 165 fraudulent (1.97%).

IMPS has the highest fraud rate, followed by NEFT, indicating the need for closer monitoring of these payment methods.

**3. Banking Channel Analysis**
- Mobile App: 20,108 transactions, 431 fraudulent (2.14%).
- Net Banking: 10,003 transactions, 201 fraudulent (2.01%).
- ATM: 9,916 transactions, 199 fraudulent (2.01%).
- Branch: 9,973 transactions, 195 fraudulent (1.96%).

Mobile App transactions show the highest fraud rate, highlighting the importance of strengthening digital banking security.

**4. Transaction Status Analysis**
- Pending: 9,991 transactions, 219 fraudulent (2.19%).
- Failed: 19,928 transactions, 418 fraudulent (2.10%).
- Success: 20,081 transactions, 389 fraudulent (1.94%).

Pending and Failed transactions show higher fraud rates compared to successful transactions and require additional investigation.

**5. Transaction Description Analysis**
- Fund Transfer: 345 fraud cases.
- Missing Description: 190 fraud cases.
- Merchant Payment: 172 fraud cases.
- Bill Payment: 165 fraud cases.
- ATM Withdrawal: 154 fraud cases.

Fund Transfers account for the highest number of fraud cases. Missing transaction descriptions also highlight a data quality issue.

**6. Fraud Timing Analysis**
The highest fraud transaction counts occur during the following hours:
- 18:00 – 56 cases.
- 13:00 – 52 cases.
- 20:00 – 51 cases.
- 15:00 – 51 cases.
- 12:00 – 49 cases.

Fraud activity is relatively higher during these hours, suggesting the need for continuous monitoring of transaction patterns throughout the day.

**7. Transaction Amount Range Analysis**
- Above 10,001: 14,801 transactions, 786 fraudulent (5.31%).
- 5,001–10,000: 12,381 transactions, 100 fraudulent (0.81%).
- 1,001–5,000: 17,160 transactions, 110 fraudulent (0.64%).
- 0–1,000: 5,658 transactions, 30 fraudulent (0.53%).

Transactions above 10,001 have a significantly higher fraud rate of 5.31%, indicating a strong relationship between higher transaction values and fraud risk.

**8. Year-wise Fraud Analysis**
- 2022–23: 2.06%.
- 2023–24: 2.10%.
- 2024–25: 1.93%.

The fraud rate increased slightly in 2023–24 before declining in 2024–25. This indicates an improvement in the latest period, although continuous fraud monitoring remains important.

### Recommendations
- **Prevent Phishing and Account Takeover:** Strengthen multi-factor authentication, transaction verification and customer awareness.
- **Monitor High-Value Transactions:** Apply additional verification to transactions above 10,001 due to their higher fraud rate.
- **Secure Digital Banking Channels:** Strengthen security controls for Mobile App and Net Banking transactions.
- **Monitor High-Risk Payment Methods:** Increase monitoring of IMPS and NEFT transactions.
- **Investigate Pending and Failed Transactions:** Identify unusual patterns, repeated failures and suspicious transaction attempts.
- **Improve Transaction Data Quality:** Correct missing transaction descriptions and unknown fraud classifications.
- **Monitor Fraud Timing Patterns:** Use time-based monitoring to identify unusual transaction activity.
- **Review Fraud Trends:** Compare fraud rates across financial years to evaluate the effectiveness of fraud prevention measures.

# 5. Device and Login Analysis

### Key Findings
- Total Registered Devices: 6,000
- Trusted Devices: 2,327
- Untrusted Devices: 2,475
- Successful Login Attempts: 15,145
- Failed Login Attempts: 14,855

### Business Insights

**1. Device Trust Analysis**
- Desktop: 939 trusted devices and 991 untrusted devices.
- Mobile: 920 trusted devices and 1,010 untrusted devices.
- Tablet: 468 trusted devices and 474 untrusted devices.

Out of 6,000 registered devices, 2,475 (approximately 41.25%) are classified as untrusted. This indicates the importance of strengthening device verification and authentication mechanisms.

**2. Login Activity Analysis**
- Successful login attempts: 15,145 (50.48%).
- Failed login attempts: 14,855 (49.52%).

Login activity is almost equally divided between successful and failed attempts. The high number of failed login attempts may indicate authentication issues or potentially suspicious login behaviour.

**3. Location-wise Failed Login Analysis**
- Ghaziabad recorded the highest number of failed login attempts, with 98 cases.
- Aurangabad followed with 95 cases.

These locations may require closer monitoring to identify unusual login patterns and potential unauthorized access attempts.

### Recommendations
- **Secure Untrusted Devices:** Introduce additional verification for untrusted devices before allowing account access.
- **Reduce Failed Login Attempts:** Investigate repeated failed login attempts to identify possible unauthorized access.
- **Monitor High-Risk Locations:** Closely monitor login activity from Ghaziabad and Aurangabad.
- **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication:** Strengthen account security through additional authentication methods.
- **Improve Device Monitoring:** Regularly review trusted and untrusted device registrations to detect unusual device activity.

# Overall Business Conclusion

The banking transaction analysis identified 1,026 fraudulent transactions out of 50,000 transactions, resulting in an overall fraud rate of 2.05% and a total fraud amount of 41.47M.

The analysis highlights several important fraud patterns, including higher fraud rates in high-value transactions, IMPS payments, Mobile App transactions, inactive accounts and certain customer segments.

It also identifies data quality issues involving missing customer information, unknown account types and incomplete transaction descriptions.

By using MySQL for data extraction and cleaning, Python for exploratory data analysis and Power BI for interactive visualization, this project provides valuable insights into banking operations and fraud patterns.

The findings can help banking institutions strengthen fraud detection, improve customer security, enhance data quality and support better risk-based decision-making.
